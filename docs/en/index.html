Pregnancy

Pregnancy mode

Weeks and days of pregnancy and an estimated due date. Pauses cycle predictions while it is on.

Stats

PDF summary

A summary of your cycles to take to your doctor. It is created on your phone and only leaves it if you share it.

Home screen

Widget

Your next period or your pregnancy weeks at a glance, with a discreet mode that shows no data.

Reminders

More reminders

A birth control reminder (pill, patch, ring or injection) and a daily reminder if you haven't logged anything. Both optional.

Stats

Redesigned stats

Summary cards, a chart of your recent cycles and short explanations of each figure.

Interface

Dark theme and English

System default, light or dark theme. The app appears in English if your phone is set to English.
