Closed testing · version 1.2.0 (build 7)

Tester script.

Thank you for testing Aura. These 14 cases cover the most important things in about 30 to 40 minutes. You can do them in any order.

0 of 14 done

Before you start

  • Use test data. You don't need to log your real cycle. If you do, keep in mind that any screenshot or file you send me may contain health data.
  • Keep Aura installed for the whole testing period: it counts toward completing the closed test.
  • Take your time with the unusual: closing the app in the middle of an action, rotating the phone, changing the time. The unexpected is the most useful.
  • There are no wrong answers. If something confuses you, even if it works, that's a finding too.
  • The app under test is in Spanish. Version 1.2.0 only has a Spanish interface (English comes in the next update). This script quotes each button with its Spanish label first and the English meaning in parentheses. Screens: Inicio (Home), Calendario (Calendar), Registrar día (Log day), Estadísticas (Stats), Ajustes (Settings).
Your progress checkmarks are saved only in this browser. If you prefer, write down your results separately.

Test cases

T01 · Installation

  1. Install Aura from the closed test link and open it.
  2. Go through the welcome pages.
Expected: There are 4 pages. The last one explains that your data lives on your phone and recommends making backups.
T02 · Logging

  1. On Home, tap "Me llegó hoy" (My period started today).
  2. Choose today.
  3. Look at the Calendar.
Expected: Only today is marked. The following days appear as estimated (dotted border). "Deshacer" (Undo) is available for about 7 seconds.
T03 · Logging

  1. Open a day and mark bleeding with an intensity.
  2. Choose a mood and one or more symptoms, and write a note.
  3. Save and open the day again.
  4. Repeat, but leave without saving after changing something.
Expected: Everything is kept when you save. If you leave with unsaved changes, Aura asks you before discarding them.
T04 · Logging

  1. With a period in progress, choose "Terminó hoy" (Ended today).
  2. Also try "Ya terminó antes" (It ended earlier) on another period.
Expected: The period is closed and any unlogged days in between are filled in. It can be reopened within 7 days.
T05 · Calendar

  1. Enter range selection mode ("Elegir varios días", Select multiple days).
  2. Mark a 4-day range.
  3. Try a range of more than 10 days.
  4. Use "Deshacer" (Undo).
Expected: The 4-day range is saved correctly. With more than 10 days, Aura asks for confirmation. "Deshacer" (Undo) restores the previous state.
T06 · Stats

  1. With fewer than 2 cycles logged, open Stats.
  2. Then log 3 or more cycles and come back.
Expected: With little data, Aura explains this instead of showing a misleading average. With more cycles, typical length, regularity and frequencies appear.
T07 · Notifications

  1. In Settings, turn on notifications and grant the permission if Android asks for it.
  2. Set the time a few minutes ahead.
  3. Check the notification on the lock screen.
  4. Turn on "Mostrar detalles en la notificación" (Show details in notifications) and compare.
  5. In a separate run, deny the permission twice.
Expected: A notification arrives with discreet text; with "Mostrar detalles en la notificación" (Show details in notifications) the text shows more. After two denials, Aura offers "Abrir ajustes" (Open settings).
T08 · Notifications

  1. With notifications turned on and scheduled, restart the phone.
  2. Wait for the time you set.
Expected: The notification still arrives, without opening Aura.
T09 · Backup

  1. In Settings, "Tus datos" (Your data), create a backup without a password and save it.
Expected: Aura warns that anyone will be able to read the file. The file is saved where you chose.
T10 · Backup

  1. Create a backup with a password of at least 10 characters.
  2. Import that file and enter a wrong password.
  3. Import it again with the correct one.
  4. After importing, use "Deshacer" (Undo).
Expected: With the wrong password, Aura shows a clear message and doesn't change your data. With the correct one, it asks for confirmation showing counts, replaces your data, and "Deshacer" (Undo) brings your previous data back.
T11 · Backup

  1. In "Tus datos" (Your data), turn on automatic backup and choose your Google account.
  2. Set the password.
  3. Check your Drive.
  4. The next day, check again.
Expected: An "Aura" folder appears with a single file. The next day there is still just one file, updated, not a new one.
T12 · Privacy

  1. Open Aura and go to the recent apps screen.
  2. Try to take a screenshot inside Aura.
  3. Turn off "Ocultar Aura en Recientes" (Hide Aura in Recents) and repeat.
Expected: With the option on, the preview is blank and the screenshot is blocked. With it off, both work.
T13 · Accessibility

  1. Increase the font size in Android settings.
  2. Go through Home, Calendar, Log day, Stats and Settings.
Expected: No text is cut off and buttons can be tapped without missing.
T14 · Data

  1. In Settings, choose "Borrar todos los datos" (Delete all data) and confirm.
  2. If you were using Drive, check your Drive afterwards.
Expected: No logs or settings remain in Aura. Files that were already in your Drive stay there, and you have to delete them yourself.

How to report a problem

What to include

  • The case number (for example T10) or the screen where it happened.
  • What you did, step by step, and what you expected.
  • What actually happened. If a message appeared, copy it exactly.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

To take a screenshot, first turn off "Ocultar Aura en Recientes" (Hide Aura in Recents) in Settings, because Aura blocks screenshots by default. Avoid showing real data.

How to classify it

BlockerData is lost or the app closes by itself.
MajorA feature doesn't do what it says.
MinorCut-off text, odd layout, confusing message.
SuggestionSomething you'd like to be different.
